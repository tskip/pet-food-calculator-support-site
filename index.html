01 How to use the app

  1. Pick your pet's type (Dog, Cat, Bird, Rabbit, Other) and give them a name.
  2. Enter the daily food amount in cups. Check the feeding guide on your food bag for the right number based on your pet's weight.
  3. Set the number of meals per day (1–10) using the stepper.
  4. Enter the total amount of food on hand, in pounds.
  5. Tap Calculate — you'll see how many days, weeks, and months your food will last.
  6. Tap Save Pet Profile to keep that pet's details for next time.

02 Frequently asked questions

How does the calculation work?

The app assumes a standard conversion of 4 cups per pound of dry food. It multiplies your total food (in pounds) by 4 to get total cups, then divides by your daily cups to get the number of days remaining.

For example: 35 lbs × 4 cups/lb = 140 cups ÷ 2.5 cups/day = 56 days.

Where do I find my pet's daily food amount?

Every bag of pet food has a feeding chart on the back or side, usually listed in cups per day based on your pet's weight. If you're unsure, your veterinarian can give you a personalized recommendation.

Does the app work for wet/canned food?

The calculator is designed for dry food measured in cups. If you're feeding wet food, you can still use it as a rough estimate by converting cans-per-day to an equivalent cup measurement — but for accuracy with mixed feeding, follow your vet's guidance.

Can I save more than one pet?

Yes. Save as many pet profiles as you like. Tap any saved pet to load their details and recalculate when you re-stock food.

How do I edit or delete a saved pet?

Edit: Tap a pet's row to load their info — make changes, then tap Update Pet Profile.

Delete one: Swipe left on a pet's row in the Saved Pets list.

Delete all: Tap Delete All Pets at the bottom of the list.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All pet profiles are stored locally on your device using iOS's secure UserDefaults storage. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. See the privacy section below for details.

I lost my saved pets after reinstalling. Why?

Because all data is stored locally, deleting the app removes your saved pets. We don't sync to the cloud — this keeps the app simple and private, but it also means you'll need to re-enter your pets if you reinstall.

Which iOS versions are supported?

Pet Food Calculator requires iOS 17 or later and runs on all current iPhone models.

03 Privacy at a glance

Pet Food Calculator collects no personal data.

  • No accounts. There's no sign-in, no email required.
  • No tracking. No analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party services.
  • Local-only storage. Your pet profiles are saved on your device using iOS UserDefaults.
  • No network calls. The app does not connect to the internet.

05 Troubleshooting

The Calculate button isn't working

Make sure both the Daily Food Amount and Total Food Available fields contain valid numbers greater than zero. The keyboard is set to a decimal pad, so use a period (not a comma) for decimal values.

The numbers look off

The calculator assumes 4 cups per pound of dry food, which is the typical density for most kibble. If your food brand publishes a different density, the result will be a slight over- or underestimate. Always defer to your veterinarian for feeding amounts.

The app feels stuck or unresponsive

Force-quit the app by swiping up from the bottom (or double-tapping Home on older devices) and swiping the app card away. Then reopen the app. If the issue persists, please email support with your iPhone model and iOS version.
